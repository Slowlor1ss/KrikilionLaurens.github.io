About Me

Currently, I'm an Engine Programmer at Climax Studios, where I primarily work on low-level engine and Audio code. My main interests are audio programming, engine programming, and performance optimization.
I enjoy working close to the engine, whether that's improving existing systems, building new functionality, or tracking down difficult performance problems.

Although engine/audio programming is my primary focus, I also enjoy gameplay programming for the creativity and variety it offers. I value working in collaborative teams where people are passionate about making great games and are always willing to learn from and help one another.

What I enjoy most about programming is solving difficult technical problems.
There's something incredibly satisfying about taking a complex challenge, finding an new and elegant solution, and seeing it all come together.
